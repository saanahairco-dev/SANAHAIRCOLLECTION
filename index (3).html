Terms & Conditions

Preorder Basis

Sana operates primarily on a preorder basis, with select items available in stock. Preorders are sourced once your deposit is received.

Payment

A 50% deposit is required to place any order. The remaining 50% is due within 7 days of your wig arriving in Zambia.

Pricing

Prices reflect raw and virgin human hair, sourced and priced transparently. Non-straight and non-body-wave textures carry a 280 ZMW surcharge.

Prices are quoted in Zambian Kwacha at the prevailing exchange rate and may adjust if that rate shifts significantly.

Order Timeline

Once customization details are confirmed, your wig is typically completed within 5–7 days, with updates throughout.

Returns & Exchanges

Wigs are inspected for quality upon arrival. If yours arrives damaged and you're not happy with it, a return is accepted. In these cases, we offer an exchange for another in-stock wig of similar price, or a new order of a similar-priced wig. Wigs that have been installed, modified, or customized in any way no longer qualify for return.

Communication

We communicate closely with clients on every detail of their wig, and offer consultations where needed to help you find the hair that best suits you. From the moment your order is placed to delivery, we keep you updated on shipping progress, transit locations, any delays, and estimated arrival times.
